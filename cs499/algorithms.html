Artifact and Origin

This stage builds on the modular CS 410 C++ application and focuses on data identity, lookup, ordered views, filtering, and updates.

Enhancement

Every client received a stable ID. An unordered_map provides average O(1) lookup and updates by ID. Temporary vectors and std::sort provide O(n log n) ordered views. Filtering performs an O(n) scan plus O(k log k) sorting for k matches.

Skills Demonstrated

Hashing, stable identifiers, vector use, comparator functions, standard-library algorithms, searching, sorting, filtering, Big-O analysis, and management of design trade-offs.

Primary evidence for Outcomes 3 and 4, with communication evidence provided by the narrative and complexity explanation.
